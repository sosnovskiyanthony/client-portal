AI is a tool, not a strategy — it's worth adding where it removes real friction from your business (a slow response, a manual process that eats hours every week, a search problem) and worth skipping where it wouldn't genuinely help. The intake and review process is built around figuring out which of those is true for you, not assuming every business needs the same AI features.
